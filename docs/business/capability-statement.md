# Capability Statement

## Executive Summary

OpenStair Technologies is a software development company based in India.

The company helps organizations design, build, integrate, and improve digital systems across mobile applications, web applications, backend APIs, and full stack software products.

This capability statement summarizes OpenStair's current business capability, technical expertise, delivery approach, and service portfolio for enterprise clients, government procurement, GeM registration, vendor onboarding, partnerships, and proposal submissions.

## Company Snapshot

- Company name: OpenStair Technologies
- Business type: Software development and product engineering services
- Core expertise: Mobile, web, backend, API integration, and full stack delivery
- Delivery model: Project-based development, product engineering support, technical consulting, and long-term software maintenance

OpenStair works with organizations that need reliable software to serve customers, support operations, automate workflows, connect systems, or launch new digital products.

## Core Capabilities

OpenStair's core capabilities include:

- Cross-platform mobile application development
- Responsive website and web application development
- Backend API design and implementation
- Authentication and authorization systems
- Database-backed application development
- Third-party service integrations
- Internal tools and operational workflows
- Technical documentation and knowledge platform development

These capabilities may be delivered independently or combined into a full product build.

## Service Portfolio

OpenStair's current service portfolio includes Flutter development, web development, backend development, API integration, and full stack product delivery.

Flutter development supports cross-platform mobile products for Android and iOS users.

Web development supports responsive websites, product interfaces, dashboards, internal tools, and web applications.

Backend development supports APIs, databases, authentication, integrations, business logic, and operational foundations.

Full stack delivery combines frontend, backend, integrations, deployment, and documentation where a complete software product is required.

## Technology Expertise

OpenStair works across the main layers required by modern software products.

Current technology capability includes Flutter, Dart, Android, Kotlin, Next.js, React, TypeScript, Spring Boot, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, Firebase, and Vercel.

Technology choices are made according to project requirements, maintainability, platform needs, team capability, security considerations, and expected system lifetime.

## Delivery Approach

OpenStair's delivery approach begins with understanding the business goal, target users, required platforms, existing systems, technical constraints, timeline, and launch expectations.

The company applies practical engineering judgment rather than unnecessary complexity. Small products may need a direct implementation with clear boundaries. Larger products may require stronger architecture, testing, documentation, deployment discipline, and operational practices.

Important technical decisions should be understandable and documented when they affect future work.

## Industries Served

OpenStair can support organizations that use software to serve customers, manage operations, publish services, automate workflows, or connect systems.

Relevant organization types include:

- Startups building initial products
- Small and medium businesses modernizing operations
- Service businesses needing customer or internal systems
- Education and training organizations
- Healthcare and wellness service providers
- Retail and local commerce businesses
- Professional service firms
- Product teams needing mobile, web, or backend engineering support

The best fit is a business problem where software can create measurable operational, customer, or product value.

## Why OpenStair

Organizations choose OpenStair when they need software delivery with practical technical judgment, clear ownership, and maintainable implementation.

OpenStair can help clarify product goals, choose an appropriate implementation path, build the required software layers, and preserve important knowledge for future work.

The company's trust factors include realistic commitments, careful engineering, transparent trade-offs, documentation discipline, and attention to long-term product usefulness.
