# About OpenStair Technologies

OpenStair Technologies is a software development company based in India.

The company designs and builds mobile applications, web applications, backend systems, API integrations, and full stack software products for businesses that need reliable digital systems.

OpenStair works with clients who need software that can support real business workflows, public users, internal teams, and future product growth. The company is service-oriented today and is building the knowledge, engineering standards, and documentation discipline needed to support future products.

## Company Role

OpenStair acts as a technical partner for organizations that need product planning, implementation, integration, and launch support.

The company helps translate business requirements into working software by combining product understanding, engineering execution, and maintainable technical foundations.

## Work Areas

- Mobile applications
- Web applications
- Backend APIs
- Database-backed systems
- Product websites
- Internal business tools
- Integrations with third-party services
