# Privacy Policy Overview

OpenStair Technologies should treat privacy as a product and business responsibility.

This document is an overview of privacy principles. It is not a complete legal privacy policy.

## Privacy Principles

- Collect only information that is needed for a clear business or product purpose.
- Explain how contact or project information is used.
- Protect submitted information from unnecessary exposure.
- Avoid sharing personal information without a valid reason.
- Review product-specific privacy needs before launch.

## Current Scope

The public website may collect project inquiry information through contact channels and forms.

Future OpenStair products may require their own privacy policy documents based on the data they collect, the users they serve, and the platforms where they are distributed.

## Relationship To Legal Documentation

This overview should support future privacy policy drafting. It should not replace formal legal review where a complete privacy policy is required.
